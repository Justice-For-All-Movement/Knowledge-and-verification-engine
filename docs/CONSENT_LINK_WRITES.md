# Current Consent checks for private source-backed links

`ConsentEvidenceLinksWriter` (`./consent-links`) requires a configured `CurrentEvidenceUse` guard and the accepted Identity/Knowledge repository, Evidence service and host-authorized Evidence credential provider. It retains the accepted source-backed capture and owning Knowledge CAS/history transaction. Its explicit guard observes current permissions before any source credential release and again after the actual owning Evidence capture, before the writer freshly resolves Knowledge Identity and begins its owning write. No denial, missing mapping or uncertain result falls back to a source-only/snapshot writer.

The fixed scoped/purpose host policy lists up to64 exact Evidence editions and their original-byte SHA256 digests, explicit rights/consent IDs (up to100 each) and literal attribution. It is copied at construction; duplicate editions, extra fields, foreign references, missing/oversized profiles and non-JSON data fail closed. The complete policy is capped at256KiB. Every requested selector must have an exact ref/digest mapping before any guard SQL. Empty link sets perform no Consent evaluation and need no source credential; they prove no evidence absence, completeness or permitted use.

For each distinct resource/action, current Identity `resolveResource` checks enabled stored human membership and an explicit `investigate` policy for `evidenceUseResourceId(exactEvidenceRef)`. Incoming roles are stripped. This is a separate opt-in Identity resource from the Knowledge history resource. The resolved current stored context is passed to the actual owning `PermissionRegistry.evaluateCurrent`, which reads its OWN registered resource requirements/current permission editions and samples trusted synchronous time after its own lock/reads. Caller subject requirements, permission records and allowed flags are never accepted. The returned exact resource digest/ref, purpose, action, private audience and observed time are checked. A missing grant, required consent, attribution or current time window denies.

Text selectors retain their literal quote, so they require `reproduce` even for an authored `supports` or `challenges` relation. Whole/page/time selectors retain reference/hash or numeric anchor metadata and require `metadata-citation`. Relation labels alone do not grant copying authority. Audience is fixed `private`; no transform, train, distribute, send or public release action is requested. Deduplication is only within one observation of the same resource/action; no decision is cached across invocations or between the pre/post observations. Historical idempotent replay still obtains current Identity/Consent/Evidence observations, then returns only the original acknowledged refs. A withdrawn permission blocks replay rather than becoming a fresh use permit.

The guard has a default10s monotonic acceptance budget configurable1..30000ms, in addition to the source writer's whole-operation budget. It gates admission and every owning-service result/copy. Begun Identity/Consent SQL is awaited; it is not raced, terminated or undone. Late, cancelled or lost acknowledgements deny further source/write admission, with sanitized callback diagnostics. A successful Knowledge write acknowledgement is still preserved under the accepted writer's rules; lost write acknowledgement remains UNKNOWN and requires explicit later reauthorization/replay. No new SQL schema, foreign DB/root access, permission mutation, stored assessment or effect route is added. Consent's SDK is explicitly pinned to accepted PR10 source cd4d35e3247aacae96c0dae7ba416125f73dc383; other pins and shared Knowledge contracts remain unchanged.

These are sequential current observations. Permissions, Identity or Evidence can change after a check, during another engine's reads or during Knowledge commit; there is no distributed revocation fence or permanent permission attached to a stored link. Consent decisions are audited in their owning registry, not converted into a transferable permit or invented Knowledge audit event. Host must authenticate human identity, authorize Evidence credential delegation, establish real rights/consent validity and subject mapping, bind original-byte digests into Consent resource registration, refresh fixed mapping instances when policy changes, protect keys and apply ingress/CPU/heap/driver/concurrency limits. Source credential principal equality is still not verified. Host/library wiring is not a network authentication service. Original-byte verification, scanner/extraction quality, production clock/time authentication, network TLS/crash/failover/browser/assistive technology and live acceptance remain separate. Direct legacy host-trust paths remain unchanged and must not be exposed as a fallback endpoint.

The new fixtures use four actual separately migrated restricted PGlite databases and encrypted temporary Evidence objects, with synthetic scanners, human mappings and permission statements. Embedded tests do not qualify independent TCP sessions, real rights ownership or informed consent. Full local/installed/export/head-CI evidence is tracked separately. AM-05.02 and the suite remain partial. PAM stops at its accepted foundation; no live providers, publication, payment or paid infrastructure are enabled.
